# Shared job review

The job Review button reads `/api/job-review` and shows a saved current review immediately. `get_job_review` in Penney MCP calls the same service. Weekly Close keeps its existing entry point.

The service uses a single database snapshot, the current contract estimate, and the app's shared historical wage/break calculator. Record fingerprints cover the whole current inventory, including older edited records and available storage object versions. Prior evidence and unresolved findings survive repeat reviews. Resolutions append a successor with source fingerprints; changed resolution evidence reopens the question.

Costs and all monetary outputs are integer cents. Explicit duplicate bills are excluded; split allocations and negative credits count once. Accepted quotes contribute only their unbilled remainder. Potentially matching unlinked bills create a reconciliation question instead of automatically adding the quote again. Current records are not a completed source-document/field audit: remaining costs, payroll burden, permits, completion and final profit stay unverified when evidence is insufficient.

## Tools

- `get_job_review`: current snapshot, saved baseline, stage, money, scopes, warnings and next three actions.
- `inspect_job_finding`: saved source records and short-lived original-document links.
- `preview_job_correction`: exact before/after allocation effects, with no financial write.
- `apply_job_correction`: applies an explicitly authorized preview through the app workflow, records before/after, then refreshes the review.
- `save_job_review_evidence`: retains inspected evidence/open issues or appends evidence-backed resolutions.

The API accepts only verified office roles (owner, office_admin, precon_manager). Browser requests require a real session, no impersonation, and the same origin. MCP uses the existing shared service key and verified caller identity; identity cannot be supplied in tool arguments. Review tables and RPCs are unavailable to public, anonymous and ordinary authenticated database callers.

Correction previews expire after 15 minutes, belong to one caller/job, and reject changed records or source versions. Invoice allocations invoke the existing `confirm_spend_review` workflow, preserving closed-line snapshots. Labor corrections require completed employee shifts and unlocked lines. Amounts, payments, budgets, clock times and job IDs cannot be changed through this feature. Retry an uncertain apply with the same preview ID; application is idempotent. Splits, cross-job transfers, new bills and payments remain in their existing workflows.

## Final audit and financial closure

The Review job focus is carried into Codex/Claude handoffs. A final-audit handoff requires the saved baseline, full source reconciliation, actual recorded overhead, a versioned report in project Files, and the existing native final-audit approval. The panel reads current closeout status through the authenticated same-origin `POST /api/finance-audit` `status` action. It links to `/finances?auditProject=<project ID>#projects`, which opens the existing AuditWorkspace. Ordinary reviews do not close projects.

The prepare action includes a proposed actual-overhead allocation and its source fingerprint. Each closed month allocates the existing company overhead pool by the job's share of signed net receipts that month. This is an explicit reviewer-accepted allocation basis, not an assumed existing global policy or the estimate planning percentage. It reuses the company report's payroll, fee and eligible-bill rules. Full lifetime job receipts establish the years; complete paginated source inventories and meaningful record fingerprints retain the calculation. Missing payroll, incomplete periods, invalid/nonpositive denominators, negative job receipts or overhead, and shares exceeding 100% block finality. A recorded payroll split establishes period coverage, not proof that every source has been audited.

Finalization accepts the displayed fingerprint and explicit acceptance, recalculates overhead on the server, and rejects changed sources. It does not accept a caller-supplied overhead/profit amount. The immutable final payload preserves monthly allocations, source fingerprints, final cost, gross profit, net profit/margin and the preexisting report, scope, evidence and collection fields. Additional payroll burden requires an explicit figure, including a verified zero. Approval must reconcile burden with direct wages and overhead to avoid duplicate payroll costs. The final record establishes financial closure; it does not trigger operational `completed` status or approve draft estimates.

The workspace compares current full job source/evidence inventories and company overhead against the saved final record. Changed/unavailable sources flag re-review and exclude the job from current closed totals, while the old P&L remains available in revision history. Reopening appends a record; no saved final is edited. All monetary access and owner/preconstruction-manager approval gates remain in place.

After verified financial closure, the assisted final-audit handoff prepares the team's results email through Penney MCP `send_email`. It uses the approved immutable figures and report, verifies internal financial-review recipients, and checks saved message IDs to avoid duplicates. The agent shows the complete parked draft and waits for fresh explicit approval before confirming the send; it rechecks that the same final record is still current. The app does not automatically send mail on finalization. Blocked, reopened or unclosed jobs do not produce final-results emails. Ordinary and Weekly Close reviews retain their no-messaging boundary.

Presence warnings for return records, separate time entries and separate ledger entries can be resolved by an exact current resolved question with complete reconciliation dependencies and a versioned project report. `prepare.coverageRequirements` returns the required title and source references. For more than 100 references, save sibling question chunks with the same exact title and the same project-file/storage-version references on every chunk; do not supersede sibling chunks. Their current references must collectively cover the complete requirement. This does not waive financial/labor errors, unresolved commitments or per-scope source verification. The current snapshot lacks material-order item rows, so active material orders remain explicitly blocked until the service can verify that inventory; headers alone cannot establish remaining obligations. A separate existing limit still blocks a verified-cost scope with more than 100 required references: the app/MCP cap and all-dependencies-per-note check must be extended together before such scopes can be finalized. Database RPC guards retain concurrency/source/evidence checks; readiness and derived P&L arithmetic are enforced by the authenticated server route.
